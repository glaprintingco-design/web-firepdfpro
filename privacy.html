Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 1, 2025

1. Introduction

FirePDF Pro ("we," "our," or "us") operates the website firepdfpro.com and the web application at app.firepdfpro.com. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our services.

By using FirePDF Pro, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you disagree, please discontinue use of the service.

2. Information We Collect

Account Information: When you register, we collect your email address and a securely hashed password. We do not store plain-text passwords.

Profile Information: You may optionally provide your contractor company name, license number, address, and contact details to pre-fill generated forms. This information is stored in your account and used solely for document generation.

Usage Data: We collect information about how you use the service, including the BIN numbers you look up, the number of forms you generate per month, and general usage patterns. This helps us maintain service quality and enforce plan limits.

Payment Information: Payments are processed through PayPal. We do not store credit card numbers or financial data on our servers. PayPal's own privacy policy governs the handling of your payment information.

Technical Data: We may collect standard server logs including your IP address, browser type, operating system, and timestamps of requests. This data is used for security, debugging, and service improvement.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the FirePDF Pro service
  • To authenticate your account and enforce subscription plan limits
  • To auto-fill FDNY forms using your saved profile information
  • To save your project history so you can revisit past jobs
  • To send transactional emails (e.g., account confirmation, password reset)
  • To detect and prevent fraud or abuse
  • To improve the service based on aggregated usage patterns

4. Generated Documents

Generated PDF documents (TM-1, A-433, B-45, Reports) are created on-demand and delivered directly to your browser for download. We do not retain copies of generated PDF files on our servers after they are delivered to you.

Project metadata (BIN number, address, job description, and device list) is saved to your account so you can track your project history.

5. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share information with trusted service providers who assist in operating our platform:

  • Supabase: Our database and authentication infrastructure provider (supabase.com).
  • PayPal: Payment and subscription processing.
  • NYC Open Data / DOB API: We query public NYC Department of Buildings data using BIN numbers you provide. No personal data is sent to this API.

We may also disclose your information if required by law, regulation, or valid legal process.

6. Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures to protect your data, including encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS), bcrypt password hashing, and Supabase's row-level security to ensure users can only access their own data.

No method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your personal information, we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

7. Data Retention

Your account data is retained for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data by emailing support@firepdfpro.com. We will process deletion requests within 30 days.

8. Cookies and Tracking

Our website may use cookies for session management and basic analytics. We do not use third-party advertising cookies. You can control cookie behavior through your browser settings.

If we use Google Analytics or similar tools in the future, we will update this policy to reflect that and provide opt-out options.

9. Children's Privacy

FirePDF Pro is intended for use by licensed fire alarm contractors and construction professionals. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18 years of age. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such data, please contact us and we will promptly delete it.

10. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete the personal data we hold about you. To exercise these rights, contact us at support@firepdfpro.com.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by posting the updated policy on this page with a new "Last updated" date. Continued use of the service after changes constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us:
